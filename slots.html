Paytable & rules

Bet 1 to 5 coins: each coin activates one line (1 middle, 2 top, 3 bottom, 4 & 5 diagonals). Three identical symbols on an active line pay the table × 1 coin. The jackpot symbol also pays when 1 or 2 of them land on a line. Space / Enter to spin, ←/→ to change the bet.
